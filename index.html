Podcast · Season 1 lineup

Podcast topics that educate, inspire, and drive action.

Every episode ends with one nugget you can use the same day. Here's what's on the mic for Season 1.

Episode 01 · Life

The 1% Rule: Why Tiny Habits Beat Big Resolutions

1.01 to the power of 365 is about 38. We unpack the math of compounding and why showing up daily beats starting over every January.

Nugget to try: pick one habit and make it 1% easier tomorrow.

Episode 02 · Money

Rich vs. a Rich Life: Define Wealth Before You Chase It

A big balance and a restless night, or a modest balance and peace of mind? A frank talk on money goals that serve the life you actually want.

Nugget to try: finish the sentence "For me, rich means…"

Episode 03 · Business

Your Expertise Is Your Content Calendar

Founders already know enough to post every day. How to turn the questions customers ask into a year of useful posts, Reels and captions.

Nugget to try: write down the last 5 questions a customer asked you.

Episode 04 · Wisdom

Boond Boond Se Saagar: Desi Proverbs for Modern Business

"Drop by drop, an ocean." Old Indian sayings that still run small businesses, from saving and patience to the power of reputation.

Nugget to try: share a proverb your grandparents lived by.

Episode 05 · Health

Three Minutes, Three Ms: Mindful, Movement, Mindset

No gym, no app, no excuses. A three-minute daily reset for busy people, and why consistency matters more than intensity.

Nugget to try: breathe for one minute, stretch for one, plan for one.

Episode 06 · Marketing

Trust Is the New Marketing: Reviews, Replies and Real Credibility

Why a thoughtful reply to a 3-star review can win more customers than an ad, and how local businesses build trust online one interaction at a time.

Nugget to try: reply to your three most recent reviews today.
