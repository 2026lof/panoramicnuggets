Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 18, 2026

You can ask Panoramic Nuggets to delete data we hold about you at any time, including data we received through Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google.

Option 1: Remove our app from Facebook or Instagram

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. On Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
  2. Find Panoramic Nuggets and select Remove.
  3. This immediately stops our access. To also delete data we already have, send us a request using Option 2.

Option 2: Email us a deletion request

Email contact@panoramicnuggets.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your name, and the email address or phone number connected to your data.
  • The business or Facebook Page you interacted with, if you know it.
  • Whether you want all data deleted or only certain information.

What happens next

  • We will confirm we received your request within 5 business days and may ask you to verify your identity.
  • We will delete your data from our systems within 30 days and send you a confirmation.
  • We may keep a minimal record of your opt-out or deletion request so we can continue to honor it, and any data the law requires us to keep.
  • If your data was collected by one of our business clients, we will delete it from our platform and notify that client so they can delete it from their own records.

Just want to stop messages? Reply STOP to any text message, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle data.
